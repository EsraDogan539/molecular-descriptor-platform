The monomer is the fused bicyclic shown in Figure 1 of the article: heteroatom X in the ring with the open
positions 4 and 6, heteroatom Y in the ring with positions 2 and 3 ("XY"; e.g. TeSe = Te at X, Se at Y). In SMILES
the system is `c1cc2c[X]cc2[Y]1` (the chalcogenopheno[3,4-b]chalcogenophene skeleton).

Level of theory as stated in the article: B3PW91 with 6-31G(d,p) for C and H and LANL2DZ (ECP) for Se and Te,
Gaussian 09/16. The polymer band gaps of the article are reported in figures only and are not transcribed.
