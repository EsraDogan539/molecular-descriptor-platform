About ChalMolDB
A curated research resource for chalcogen-focused molecular data, designed around transparent identity, provenance and reusable scientific descriptors.

Replacing sulfur with selenium or tellurium is one of the simplest ways to tune the band gap of donor–acceptor molecules for organic solar cells and transistors, but the relevant data are scattered across many studies with different methods. ChalMolDB collects these records in one place, with standardized structures and the source and level of theory of every value.

Each record keeps its standardized structure (canonical SMILES, InChI and InChIKey), its electronic properties and interpretable S/Se/Te annotations, so that records can be inspected, compared and reused in reproducible cheminformatics workflows. Predictive modelling is a secondary use case rather than the primary purpose of the database.

HOW TO CITE

If you use ChalMolDB, please cite the database release:

Doğan, E. N.; Kayı, H. ChalMolDB: Chalcogen Molecular Database, Database v1.1. Zenodo, 2026. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22903549

The concept DOI covers all versions and resolves to the latest one; when reporting results, cite the version DOI of the release you used (Database v1.1: 10.5281/zenodo.23238884). A journal article describing ChalMolDB is in preparation.
