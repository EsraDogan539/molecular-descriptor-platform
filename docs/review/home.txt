ABOUT CHALMOLDB
Find structures, compare electronic properties, trace provenance, and reproduce your queries in one chalcogen-focused research environment.
ChalMolDB is a curated research platform for sulfur-, selenium- and tellurium-containing molecular systems.
What is ChalMolDB?

A searchable, structure-aware database for systematic exploration of chalcogen-focused molecular data.

Who is it for?

Researchers in computational chemistry, cheminformatics, organic electronics and materials informatics working with S/Se/Te-containing molecules.

Where does the data come from?

Records are curated from documented computational and literature-derived sources, with original provenance retained at record level.

Why is it useful?

Search molecular motifs, compare structures, filter HOMO/LUMO/Eg values, inspect provenance, and replay saved queries.
