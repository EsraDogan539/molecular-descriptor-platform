Unique-structure and repeated-group counts use standardized InChIKey identity where an exact structure is available. Of the 367 repeated-structure groups, 105 are in the development collection (records that share a standardized structure in the source library), 94 in the external collection, where the same oligomer is reported at more than one level of theory or set of conditions and 154 in the polymer collection (polymers whose truncated oligomers coincide), 14 in the literature collection (the same molecule computed with several functionals). Reference-backed records contain a stored source/reference value. Parsed DOI records: 5,029.
